# Roadmap Builder 1.5.0

The biggest release so far: structure for larger plans, two ways to present, a new look, and every guide
and video redone to match.

## New in 1.5.0

**Plan**

- **Levels.** Activities can be nested inside a workstream, up to four levels deep: Ctrl+] puts a row under the row above it, Ctrl+[ moves it back. A parent row folds, and its bar and dates are worked out from the rows beneath it.
- **Dependencies.** Drag from the dot at the end of a bar to the row that has to wait for it. Lines show for the bar under the pointer and the selected bar; the Links eye shows them all. A line turns red when an activity starts before the one it waits for has ended. Nothing is moved automatically.
- **Templates.** Templates, on the toolbar, starts from a simple, a typical or a complex roadmap. Undo brings the previous roadmap back.

**Present**

- **Pointer tracking.** The target on the toolbar lets the pointer steer: the timeline glides towards the edge the pointer is near, the wheel zooms, and a click on a row folds its workstream.
- **Slides.** Save views of the roadmap as slides, in Edit mode, on a new Slides tab, each with a title and an optional description.
- **Presentation mode.** The screen on the toolbar glides from one saved view to the next, forwards and back, over a set number of seconds. A thin bar across the top of the window fills as you go.
- **Slides as a PDF.** One A4 page per slide, with the slide's number, title and description as a caption.

**Find and see**

- **Commands.** Ctrl+K or Ctrl+Space opens one searchable list of what the app can do, in groups, and finds the names on the roadmap.
- **Version on the toolbar.** The app shows which version you are using, beside Guide.
- **A toolbar that fits.** On a narrow window the buttons that do not fit move behind a "…" button instead of scrolling off the side.
- **Row height and bar thickness.** From Compact to Extra tall, with thin, standard or thick bars.

**Look**

- **Open Sans.** The default font is now Open Sans, with no letter-spacing on headings. A roadmap saved earlier that still used the old default, Barlow, opens in Open Sans; Barlow is still in the list.
- **Header styles.** Label tab, Solid strip, Glass strip, Underline and Two-tone are one slim, square line; Classic keeps the taller bar.
- **Eyes that stay put.** Every eye keeps its place: solid when shown, a faint outline when hidden. A hidden date column or switched-off floating cards stay on screen in Edit mode, dimmed. This also fixes hidden eyes jumping to the left of their row.

**Guides and videos**

- **The Guide has seven chapters.** Two are new: Levels, links and templates; and Present. The other five cover the new commands, eyes, row heights and header styles.
- **Fourteen guide videos** (seven chapters, light and dark) and the **product film, now in eight chapters**, were recorded again from this version.

## Changed, and worth knowing

- The roadmap file is now version 4. Older files open as before and are brought up to date.
- In Edit mode the hover card lets the pointer through, so it cannot sit over a bar. Drag it to a new place, or reset it, in View mode.
- If an activity with dates is given rows beneath it, its own dates are kept in the file but not shown while it is a parent.
- The CSV activity list does not carry levels, links or slides. The roadmap file does.
- The release pipeline stops if the tag does not match the version shown in the app.

## Known limits

- Tested in Chrome only. Pointer tracking and the wheel zoom were tested with scripted pointer movement, not with a trackpad.
- The guide videos and the film are narrated by a synthetic voice; nobody has listened to this recording.
- Nothing found by the 1.4.4 quality report has been fixed: jsPDF 2.5.1 and the container's base image still have open advisories.
- In the film, the Guide dialog shows the steps without videos, because the film is recorded from a copy of the app that has no guide videos beside it.
- Slides do not hide floating cards on screen, so cards can bunch up when a slide folds everything. The PDF leaves them off.
- The zip does not yet include `LICENSE` or `THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md`.

# Roadmap Builder 1.3.0

A new layout, a Properties panel, and several new ways to look at and share a roadmap.

## New

- **Full-window schedule.** The schedule fills the window and scrolls on its own, up and down and sideways, with its headings and the name column pinned. The toolbar floats above it.
- **Properties panel.** A full-height panel on the right holds the selected item's settings, Add, Text and Display, with the panel cards on a Cards tab. The Properties button hides and shows it.
- **Label size and date size** are set separately, from 80% to 130%.
- **JSON editor as a full-height panel on the left**, with line numbers. A mistake is reported with its line, and that line is marked. Ctrl+S, the Format button or leaving the editor lays the text out again. Floating cards and panel cards are listed too, one line each.
- **Resize the timeline on the timeline.** Drag the year scale to squeeze or stretch it; double-click to fit the window.
- **Fit the timeline to a row.** Right-click a row, workstream or critical path stage: the timeline is scaled and scrolled so that row fills the middle 80% of the screen.
- **Pin floating cards to rows.** A pinned card moves with its row, so it is still in the right place when rows are hidden in View mode.
- **Image of this view.** The image export now shows the timeline as it is on screen: the same zoom, starting where it is scrolled to. "Image, whole timeline" exports everything.
- **Standalone page, view only.** One html file locked to View mode that opens exactly as the roadmap looks at export.
- **Help opens in its own window.** The list of requirements moved to `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md`.

## Changed

- The full settings of an item now open in the Properties panel instead of under its row. Quick edits (name, dates, colour, show or hide) are still on the rows.
- The Display and Add bars at the top are gone; their controls are in Properties.

| `roadmap-builder-guide-videos.zip` | The ten guide videos on their own (five chapters, light and dark, 3840 x 2160), with subtitles |

| `roadmap-builder-film-1080p.mp4` | The product film, smaller copy for sharing |

| `roadmap-builder-film-chapters.txt` | The film's chapters and their start times |
| `SHA256SUMS.txt` | Checksums of the files above |

## Known limits

- The guide videos and the film are narrated by a synthetic voice; nobody has listened to this recording.
- The film's Export menu does not show "Standalone page, view only", which was added after it was recorded.
- Tested in Chrome only.

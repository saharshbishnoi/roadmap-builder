# Roadmap Builder 1.4.4

The app is the same as 1.4.3. This release adds a quality report to the build and attaches it here, along
with new documents for anyone changing the app.

## New in 1.4.4

- **Quality report.** `./quality/run.sh` makes one page of figures: known vulnerabilities (CVEs) with an inventory of what is packaged, static analysis, and how much of the app the browser tests run. It reports only and cannot stop a build. It lives in its own `quality/` folder and changes nothing in the app.
- **Attached to the release.** The pipeline runs the report and attaches `roadmap-builder-quality-report.zip`. Unzip it and open `index.html`.
- **New documents.** `docs/DEVELOPMENT.md` (development and integration), `docs/TESTING.md` (how the app is tested) and `docs/VIDEO-PROMPT.md` (the brief for the videos).
- **README.** A link to the hosted app and pictures of it in light and dark at the top.

## What the first report found

Nothing has been fixed yet; these are for the next release.

- **jsPDF 2.5.1**, used for PDF export, has 2 critical and 8 high advisories; fixes are in 3.0.x and 4.x. Whether the app's use of it is affected has not been assessed.
- **The container's base image**, `nginx:1.27-alpine`, has many advisories with fixes available. This affects the container only, not the zip, the hosted site or standalone pages.
- **Tests run 65.5% of the functions and 62.4% of the branches** in `app.js`.
- **Static analysis: 108 findings**, most of them rule ordering in the stylesheet and unused variables in `app.js`.

## Known limits

- The quality report step has not run in the pipeline before this release. It is set so that a failure leaves the release without the report rather than stopping it.
- The guide videos and the film are narrated by a synthetic voice; nobody has listened to this recording.
- In the film, the Guide dialog shows the steps without videos, because the film is recorded from a copy of the app that has no guide videos beside it.
- The zip does not yet include `LICENSE` or `THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md`; it carries the shorter `vendor/THIRD-PARTY.txt` and the font licence texts.
- Tested in Chrome only.
